About the Workshop

Building culturally aware multilingual AI systems.

There are over 7,000 languages spoken globally, yet only a small number are considered high-resource languages in modern Large Language Model development.


This workshop explores how multilingual and multicultural aware LLMs can better support low-resource languages, oral traditions, indigenous knowledge systems, dialectal diversity, and culturally grounded communication practices.

Workshop Goals

  • Improve collaboration across global AI and low-resource language research communities.
  • Advance multilingual and multicultural aware LLM research.
  • Create mentorship and networking opportunities.
  • Promote responsible and culturally aware AI systems.
  • Support early-career researchers and independent researchers.
