About the Workshop

Following the successful first CLRLC–LLMs Workshop @ NeurIPS 2025 held in person in Mexico City, which focused on scalable methods for low-resource data creation, model alignment, as well as discussions around language endangerment and linguistic inclusion in LLMs, the second CLRLC–LLMs Workshop aims to bring together diverse researchers from academia and industry, linguists, technologists, AI/ML engineers, open-source contributors, and NLP/AI communities working on low-resource languages to discuss the development, evaluation, and responsible deployment of multilingual and multicultural aware LLMs. This workshop will provide a platform for participants to share the challenges they face in building culturally inclusive and linguistically aware LLMs, the solutions and methodologies they have explored, current limitations in multilingual systems, and the open problems that remain unsolved in developing diverse and inclusive AI for low-resource language communities.


Workshop Goals

  • To improve collaboration across global AI and low-resource language research communities.
  • To create networking and mentorship opportunities for early-career researchers, students, and underrepresented communities working in multilingual NLP and low-resource language technologies.
  • To bring together speakers and experts working on multilingual and multicultural aware LLMs to share insights, experiences, research findings, and practical challenges from academia, industry, and community-driven initiatives.
  • To provide a space for independent researchers, students, and emerging researchers to showcase their work, ongoing research, datasets, tools, and ideas related to multilingual and low-resource LLM research.
  • To promote responsible, equitable, and culturally aware multilingual LLMs across diverse global communities.

Topics of Interest

We welcome contributions from NLP, linguistics, computational linguistics, cognitive science communities, as well as from practitioners and technologists working at the intersection of language, culture, and artificial intelligence. Submissions may include technical or interdisciplinary work in areas such as:

  • Computational Linguistics
  • NLP for Low-Resource Languages
  • Culturally Aware AI Systems
  • Speech, ASR, and Machine Translation
  • Evaluation and Benchmarking for Low-Resource Languages
  • Human–AI Interaction and Linguistic Inclusivity
  • New Techniques for Improving AI Models and LLMs in Low-Resource Settings

Contact & Organising Institution

Center for Low-Resource Languages and Cultures (CLRLC)

This workshop is organised and supported by the Center for Low-Resource Languages and Cultures (CLRLC), a global initiative advancing research, technology, and community-building for low-resource languages and culturally inclusive AI systems. CLRLC works across multilingual NLP, linguistic inclusion, AI accessibility, and culturally grounded language technologies through collaborations with researchers, students, AI/ML engineers, open-source communities, and institutions worldwide.
